# Gazetteer Overview and Methodology (10000BC - 1300AD)

## Structure of the 142-Settlement Dataset

This companion document details the settlement-level gazetteer
accompanying the regional demographic synthesis of Chernigov and
Novgorod-Seversk. Book-ended at the Mongol Conquests (1239AD) and the
immediate post-conquest devolved principalities (1240AD - 1300AD), the
gazetteer catalogues 142 verified archaeological type-sites, Severian
and Radimichi promontory hillforts, tenth-century Varangian-Rus retinue
complexes, and Olgovichi chronicle towns (*gorodishcha*) across the 72
235 km² Southern Core of the Chernigov-Seversk realm (Chernihivska
Oblast, Sumska Oblast, and the Eastern Gomel and Lower Sozh corridor).

Each settlement entry records its canonical English and historical
designation along with Ukrainian, Belarusian, and Old East Slavic
chronicle orthographies, verified geographic coordinates in decimal
degrees (between 50,14°N - 53,13°N and 29,83°E - 35,26°E), functional
settlement classification (regional capital, principality capital,
chronicle fortress, or archaeological site), and benchmark population
series across nineteen chronological horizons from 10000BC to 1300AD
(10000BC, 6000BC, 4000BC, 1500BC, 500BC, 0AD, 300AD, 500AD, 700AD,
800AD, 900AD, 1000AD, 1050AD, 1100AD, 1150AD, 1200AD, 1239AD, 1250AD,
and 1300AD). Unoccupied horizons are omitted rather than encoded as
zero.

## Calibration Multipliers Across Pre-Mongol Horizons (10000BC - 1300AD)

For the Upper Palaeolithic to Early Iron Age (10000BC - 500AD,
comprising 22 type-sites), mammoth-hunter winter base camps on the
middle Desna (Mezin, Pushkari I, Chulatovo I-II, Novgorod-Seversky, and
Buzhany) are scaled at 75 - 120 persons per multi-dwelling bone-hut
complex
[\[1\]](#ref-shovkoplyas1965mezinskaya)[\[2\]](#ref-boriskovsky1953paleolit)[\[3\]](#ref-velichko1997mammoth).
Yukhnovo and Milograd Early Iron Age promontory hillforts (0,5 - 3,0 ha)
are calibrated at 380 - 780 inhabitants
[\[4\]](#ref-tretyakov1966finno), while Late Zarubintsy, Kiev culture
(Roishche and Harivka-Desna), and Kolochin (Kolochin I) riverside
complexes are scaled at 580 - 850 inhabitants
[\[5\]](#ref-byrnya1991ranneslavyanskie)[\[6\]](#ref-symonovich1983chernyakhov).

For the Volyntsevo, Romny-Severian, and Viking-Age centres (700AD -
1000AD), major Severian and Radimichi *opoliya* citadels (Volyntsevo,
Bytytsia, Gornal, Novotroitske, and Romny) and Varangian-Rus retinue
complexes (Shestovytsia at 3 200, Boldyni Hory at 2 500, Mokhov at 1
950, and Lyubech at 3 500) are reconstructed from excavated
sunken-dwelling (*poluzemlyanka*) densities and barrow necropolis
mortality tables
[\[7\]](#ref-sukhobokov1975slaviane)[\[8\]](#ref-motsia2011shestovytsia)[\[9\]](#ref-androschuk2013vikings).

For the Olgovichi principalities at their twelfth- and early
thirteenth-century apogee (1050AD - 1239AD), fortified *detinets* and
*okolny gorod* ramparts (120 - 150 inh/ha) and unfortified *posad* and
*podol* craft suburbs (75 - 100 inh/ha) follow the archaeological
syntheses of V. P. Kovalenko [\[10\]](#ref-kovalenko2008chernihiv), A.
V. Kuza [\[11\]](#ref-kuza1989malye)[\[12\]](#ref-kuza1996gorodishcha),
P. P. Tolochko [\[13\]](#ref-tolochko1989goroda), A. K. Zaitsev
[\[14\]](#ref-zaitsev1975chernigov), Yu. Yu. Morgunov
[\[15\]](#ref-morgunov2003posemye), and H. V. Shtykhov
[\[16\]](#ref-shtykhov1992goroda). For the October 1239AD Mongol
destruction and post-conquest devolved principalities (1250AD - 1300AD),
population estimates are calibrated from excavated destruction horizons
across the Posemye and Desna corridors (Putivl, Vyr, Vorgol, Vsevolozh,
and Chernigov) and the post-conquest survival of Hlukhiv (4 500 in
1300AD), Novgorod-Seversk (4 500), Putivl (3 800), Gomel (3 400), and
Chernigov (6 000)
[\[17\]](#ref-dimnik2003olgovichi)[\[18\]](#ref-kuczynski1936ziemie).
