# Introduction

This gazetteer compiles the geographical coordinates, historical and
indigenous names, occupation spans, and benchmark population histories
of 193 individual settlements across Western Siberia (the Ob-Irtysh
river basin, ~2,40M km²) from 6000BC to 1900AD
[\[1\]](#ref-Chernykh2022). Reading directly from the regional
settlement dataset in the standard Stadester format, the compilation
covers prehistoric, Bronze Age, and Iron Age settlements across the
Altai region, the South-West Siberian Steppe and Forest-Steppe, and the
North-West Siberian Taiga, Tundra, and Yugra, followed by medieval
Ob-Ugrian, Selkup, Turkic, and Siberian Tatar fortified centres
(*gorodki* and *gorodishcha*), and early modern to Imperial Russian
towns, *ostrogi*, factory settlements (*zavody*), and *slobody*.

Prehistoric, Bronze Age, and Iron Age settlement populations are
estimated from excavated house counts, dwelling floor areas
($`4,5\text{ m}^2`$ per person with a contemporaneity factor of $`0,65`$
to $`0,80`$), and geomagnetic settlement plans
[\[2\]](#ref-Piezonka2023)[\[3\]](#ref-Dubovtseva2020)[\[4\]](#ref-Borzunov2012)[\[5\]](#ref-Ryabogina2010)[\[6\]](#ref-Outram2009)[\[7\]](#ref-Epimakhov2009)[\[8\]](#ref-Krause2019)[\[9\]](#ref-Molodin2009)[\[10\]](#ref-Matveeva1999)[\[11\]](#ref-Losey2018)[\[12\]](#ref-Grushin2011)[\[13\]](#ref-Dashkovskiy2024).
Medieval Yugra and Siberian Khanate populations combine excavated
*gorodishche* house counts with 17th-century Russian fur-tribute
(*yasak*) registers using B. O. Dolgikh’s household multipliers ($`4,0`$
per adult male payer for Ob-Ugrian and Selkup communities and $`4,5`$ to
$`5,0`$ for Siberian Tatar *yurts*)
[\[14\]](#ref-Dolgikh1960)[\[15\]](#ref-Bakhrushin1935)[\[16\]](#ref-Kardash2009)[\[17\]](#ref-Vizgalov2008)[\[18\]](#ref-Vizgalov2011)[\[19\]](#ref-Solodkin2015)[\[20\]](#ref-Tataurov2016)[\[21\]](#ref-Maslyuzhenko2018)[\[22\]](#ref-Matveev2011)[\[23\]](#ref-Vodyasov2016)[\[24\]](#ref-Vodyasov2017)[\[25\]](#ref-Gorbunov2018).
Early modern and 18th- to 19th-century urban and *sloboda* populations
are drawn from 17th-century garrison and household rosters (*dozornye*
and *perepisnye knigi*), the ten Imperial Poll-Tax Revisions (1719-1858)
[\[26\]](#ref-Kabuzan1990)[\[27\]](#ref-Kolesnikov1973)[\[28\]](#ref-Rezun1989)[\[29\]](#ref-Korusenko2011),
and the First All-Russian Imperial Census of 1897
[\[30\]](#ref-Patkanov1911)[\[31\]](#ref-Tychinskikh2019)[\[32\]](#ref-Bryukhanova2021).
