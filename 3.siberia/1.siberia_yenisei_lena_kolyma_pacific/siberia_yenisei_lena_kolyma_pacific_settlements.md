# Introduction

This supplementary report presents the tabular settlement gazetteer
accompanying the regional demographic review of Eastern and Pacific
Siberia (10000BC - 1900AD; Figure ). The regional dataset follows the
flat Stadester structure, indexing r length(gaz_data) individual
settlements by name and country with six standardised fields (name,
alternate names, coordinates, country, key, and population) sorted in
chronological order (Tables to ). Active occupation spans correspond
directly to the first and last recorded benchmark years in each
settlement’s population history.

Prehistoric camp and pit-house village figures are derived from
excavated dwelling counts, floor areas, and cemetery sizes
[\[1\]](#ref-Chernykh2022)[\[2\]](#ref-Weber2016a)[\[3\]](#ref-Kuzmin2006)[\[4\]](#ref-AlekseyevDyakonov2009)[\[5\]](#ref-Fitzhugh2016)[\[6\]](#ref-Dneprovskiy2017).
Estimates for Iron Age and medieval fortified settlements
(*gorodishcha*) in Transbaikalia, Tuva, Khakassia, the Amur Basin, and
Primorye are drawn from intramural areas and excavated house-pit totals
[\[7\]](#ref-Vasyutin2022)[\[8\]](#ref-Tulush2011)[\[9\]](#ref-Tulush2016)[\[10\]](#ref-Artemyeva2013)[\[11\]](#ref-Artemyeva2025)[\[12\]](#ref-Gelman2018)[\[13\]](#ref-Ivliev2021).
Early Russian fortresses (*ostrogi*), 18th-century mining and border
towns, and 19th-century urban centres are compiled from *yasak* and
garrison rolls
[\[14\]](#ref-Dolgikh1960)[\[15\]](#ref-Chertykov2009)[\[16\]](#ref-Ushnitskiy2017a)[\[17\]](#ref-Turaev2013),
imperial revision lists and historical urban studies
[\[18\]](#ref-Kabuzan1985)[\[19\]](#ref-Mansheev2010)[\[20\]](#ref-Kurchatova2015)[\[21\]](#ref-Kyshpanakov2022a)[\[22\]](#ref-Kolomiets2022)[\[23\]](#ref-Rygalova2024),
and the 1897 First All-Russian Imperial Census
[\[24\]](#ref-Troinitskiy1905)[\[25\]](#ref-Patkanov1912).
