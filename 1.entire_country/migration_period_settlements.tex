\section{Overview and Estimation
Rules}\label{overview-and-estimation-rules}

This supplementary gazetteer compiles \texttt{198} excavated Migration
Period (\texttt{c.\ 250-750AD}) and Early Medieval transitional
settlements, promontory hillforts (\emph{gorodishcha}), riverine
villages (\emph{selishcha}), and fortified towns across the six
historical regions of Russia. All records are stored in
\texttt{migration\_period\_settlements.json} in the standard
\texttt{population\_Stadester} dictionary format, keyed by
\texttt{\textless{}Name\textgreater{}-Russia} with English and Russian
names, decimal coordinates (\texttt{coords}), and positive integer
population estimates (\texttt{population}) keyed by calendar year.

For each settlement, population figures during the Migration Period
(\texttt{250-750AD}) and the Early Medieval transition (\texttt{900AD})
are derived from excavated dwelling counts, fortified and unfortified
habitation areas, and associated cemetery burial totals
\citeproc{ref-oblomskiy2016kolochinskaya}{{[}1{]}}\citeproc{ref-krenke2019moskvorechye}{{[}2{]}}\citeproc{ref-goldina1999drevnyaya}{{[}3{]}}\citeproc{ref-gadzhiev2002derbent}{{[}4{]}}.
In the JSON file, only inhabited benchmark years
(\texttt{population\ \textgreater{}\ 0}) are recorded so that the first
and last keys represent each site's documented occupation span; in the
tables below, benchmarks prior to foundation or after abandonment are
denoted by \texttt{-}. Where a Migration Period settlement evolved
directly into a modern town or village on the same site, its population
at the First All-Russian Census of \texttt{1897} (\texttt{1900AD}
benchmark) is included \citeproc{ref-troinitsky1905perepis}{{[}5{]}}.
