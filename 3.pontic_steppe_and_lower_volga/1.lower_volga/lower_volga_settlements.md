# Overview

This supplementary report presents the tabular settlement gazetteer
accompanying the regional demographic review of the Lower Volga
(10000BC - 1900AD). The dataset contains 146 individual settlements
across the Volga Delta, Volga-Akhtuba Floodplain, Saratov-Volgograd
river corridor, Kalmyk Steppe, and Astrakhan-Volgograd Trans-Volga from
10000BC to 1900AD, indexing each settlement with alternate and
historical names, decimal coordinates, and chronological population
estimates for inhabited benchmark years.

Population figures are derived from excavated pit-house floor areas and
cemetery cohorts for Mesolithic, Neolithic, Eneolithic, Bronze Age
Srubnaya and Sarmatian sites
[\[1\]](#ref-koltsov2005mezolit)[\[2\]](#ref-smagin2018mikrolitov)[\[3\]](#ref-yudin2004varfolomeyevka)[\[4\]](#ref-baratskov2015itogi)[\[5\]](#ref-vybornov2022khronologiya)[\[6\]](#ref-agapov1990khvalynskiy)[\[7\]](#ref-tsibin2019maksimovka)[\[8\]](#ref-malov2008nizhneye)[\[9\]](#ref-lopatin2014nachalo)[\[10\]](#ref-skripkin2012chislennosti)[\[11\]](#ref-balabanova2009khronologicheskiye),
urban and rural settlement excavations for Khazar Itil, Pre-Mongol
Saksin and Golden Horde cities
[\[12\]](#ref-solovyev2022semibugry)[\[13\]](#ref-vasilyev2015saksin)[\[14\]](#ref-vasilyev2020samosdelka)[\[15\]](#ref-zilivinskaya2014ocherki)[\[16\]](#ref-yegorov1985geografiya)[\[17\]](#ref-fyodorovdavydov1994goroda)[\[18\]](#ref-nedashkovsky2000ukek)[\[19\]](#ref-nedashkovsky2010goroda)[\[20\]](#ref-nedashkovsky2022predmety)[\[21\]](#ref-shigapov2022selishch)[\[22\]](#ref-zeleneev2018selitrennom)[\[23\]](#ref-pigarev2021topografii)
and Russian garrison rolls, Imperial tax revisions (*revizii*), Volga
German colony censuses, Kalmyk and Inner (Bukey) Kazakh Horde records
and the 1897 Census
[\[24\]](#ref-zaitsev2006astrakhanskoye)[\[25\]](#ref-syzranov2014astrakhani)[\[26\]](#ref-trepavlov2016nogayskoy)[\[27\]](#ref-moiseev2015stepnyye)[\[28\]](#ref-kabuzan1963narodonaseleniye)[\[29\]](#ref-solovyov2013saratovskoy)[\[30\]](#ref-brylyov2018volgogradskoy)[\[31\]](#ref-pleve1998nemetskiye)[\[32\]](#ref-doenninghaus2011nemetskikh)[\[33\]](#ref-ochirov2008tyurkoyazychnyye)[\[34\]](#ref-okonova2012migratsii)[\[35\]](#ref-kyshpanakov2026kalmyki)[\[36\]](#ref-belousov2019bukeyevskoy)[\[37\]](#ref-pirova2020tatarakh)[\[38\]](#ref-troynitsky1904saratov)[\[39\]](#ref-troynitsky1904astrakhan).
